# Starship: what it must never become

1. Something that acts outward without your word.
   No sending, posting, paying or merging a failing change unless you said so.
2. A dark factory.
   You decide what gets built and approve what ships.
   Its job is the middle.
3. Something that mixes your worlds.
   Personal, work and team stay apart: accounts, data and files.
4. A way your private life leaks into shared code.
   Your own tools live in your private add-on, never in what the team uses.
5. Something that forces one person's setup on another.
   Yours working doesn't mean someone else's will.
6. A system that changes its own rules quietly.
   Its rules change only through a reviewed change you approve.
7. Machinery for its own sake.
   As complex as it needs to be, but simple enough that it works.
8. Something you have to learn to understand.
   It talks in outcomes, not internals.
