## Note on the "inbox read-only" step above
That fixture was ineffective: deliver's ensure_dirs chmods the inbox back to 0700 before writing, so the
mailbox write really succeeded ("pending" lists it and its transcript is "The whole ten minute note about the
finances PR"). The recording was removed only because its words were saved. The genuine mailbox-cannot-save
case is in cli-retry-mailbox-failure-keeps-recording.txt (the inbox replaced by a plain file) and in
test_desk_voice_retry_keeps_the_recording_when_the_mailbox_cannot_save; in both the recording stays saved.
