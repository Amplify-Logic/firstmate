## Detection limits

- An in-place edit older than the 86400s revision window is not detected.
- A reply on a thread whose parent is not in the tracked set can appear in no cursor read.
- The tracked set is bounded, not forever: a parent whose reply marker has not advanced within 86400s leaves it, and replies on a parent that has left are not re-read.
- Routine traffic older than 86400s leaves the polled set and is listed by `items --state inactive`. Nothing is deleted, and nothing owed, waiting or corrected ever leaves.
- The 1800s poll interval is a target detection latency, not a guaranteed upper bound: sleep, offline stretches, backoff and queue delay all add to it.
- Measured detection latency so far: 2 sample(s), mean 48024s, max 94054s
